# Selected Portuguese localization

English is the default at `/`; use `lang="en"`. Future human-reviewed Portuguese pages live under `/pt/`. Translate selected complete pages after the English experience is stable; full parity is not required. Keep navigation labels and contact prompts coherent within each translated page.

Each published translation has a self-referencing canonical. Add reciprocal `hreflang="en"` and `hreflang="pt-BR"` links only when both actual routes exist; `x-default` points to the English equivalent. Do not canonicalize Portuguese pages to English. Keep translated entry relationships explicit with a stable translation key when content is added.

Do not add an empty language switcher, unreviewed machine translations, dead `/pt/` routes, or hreflang pointing to missing pages. Publication approval and confidentiality review apply independently to translated wording.
